§01 The Firm Jarod, Vadodara

Small firm. Short line between you and the answer.

Panchal Gases trades industrial gas cylinders out of Jarod, Vadodara — five product lines, standard cylinder sizes, and multiple purity grades on argon and nitrogen. Buyers range from fabrication workshops and welding contractors to other industrial and commercial units.

It's a newer operation, and it's built around one thing staying true as it grows: when you call, you're speaking to the proprietor. Not a call centre, not a chain of middlemen. Stock, grade and price get confirmed with you directly, every time.

      §06 Handling Read before use

      Four things, every time

      Store upright, store secured

      Cylinders are under pressure. Keep them vertical, chained or chocked, and clear of heat and direct sun.

      Trained hands only

      Fitting and handling should be done by trained personnel following standard safe-handling practice.

      Check the grade before you strike

      Especially on TIG and MIG work — confirm the grade you've received is the one your process needs.

      Ask for the paperwork

      If your procurement process needs specific safety documentation, say so when you enquire and we'll sort it.
